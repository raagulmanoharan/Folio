# Jarwiz / a new first impression

## Style prompt
An entirely redesigned marketing-demo interface, using Jarwiz's canvas, intention-first onboarding and AI ideation as the product reference. The user's latest instruction replaces the earlier requirement to reproduce the existing controls. Keep the approved portfolio's white, charcoal and Neue Montreal identity. Three continuous states within one precise application window: a personal starting point, a focused prompt, and three visibly different idea cards. Daymark's onboarding problem stays consistent. These are authored demo concepts, not footage of shipped software.

## Colors
- #f5f5f2: outer stage; #ffffff: application surfaces.
- #171918: primary ink and controls; #656d68: secondary text.
- #dfe3df: rules; #edf0ed: inset surfaces.
- #2563eb: focus; #059669: completed state.

## Typography
Local Neue Montreal Regular and Medium. At1920px: headings64–70px, card headings38px, body30–34px, secondary UI24–28px. No UI text smaller than20px. Natural wrapping and concise labels.

## Motion
22seconds. One persistent application window. Interior screens crossfade over0.6seconds. Persona selection resolves before Continue. Type the prompt once. Camera settles before cards populate; stagger by0.16seconds and hold all three for at least3seconds. One intentional user pointer. Deterministic timeline.

## What not to do
- No embedding the old app, reproducing its modal, or recording its camera behavior.
- No clipped chrome, orphan controls, or off-frame text.
- No tiny full-screen screenshots inside cards; show enlarged useful fragments.
- No invented customer results, fake live connections, random or endless motion.
- No edits to the portfolio or the other13films during this iteration.
