A few things to know.

A little more about working with Jarwiz.

What can I bring to a board?

Start with notes, links, images, PDFs, or spreadsheets. Arrange them around the question you’re exploring, and select the pieces you want Jarwiz to work with.

Can I keep editing what Jarwiz makes?

Yes. Work on the cards and documents, ask follow-up questions, or leave a comment on the part you want to change. The earlier material stays on the board as the project develops.

How can I try it?

Request an invitation by email. Access is being prepared for a closed beta, and invitations will be sent personally. You can describe the kind of project you’d like to bring.
